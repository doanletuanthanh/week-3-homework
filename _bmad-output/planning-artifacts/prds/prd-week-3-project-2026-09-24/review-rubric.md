# PRD Quality Review — PRD: Phòng tập phỏng vấn người dùng (tên tạm)

Reviewed: `prd.md` + `addendum.md` (2026-09-24). Stakes: production MVP, 1 dev, ~2 weeks, public launch. Reviewer feedback being addressed: replay mechanical spec, ≤2 LLM calls/turn, narrower MVP.

## Overall verdict
This is a strong PRD for its stakes. It has a clear thesis ("evidence of where you went wrong"), an honest cut table that names consequences, launch acceptance criteria that can be tested, and a 2-call engine whose trust boundary is clearly argued (§2, §5.2, addendum §5). All three reviewer concerns get a substantive answer. The main risk is in the part the reviewer cared about most. The replay promises "3 lượt qua cùng cổng", but the hook-ledger rules leave only the first replay turn able to unlock a follow-up target. Separately, the addendum's schedule math (13.75/14 days, unverified) recommends launching with one scenario, and the PRD body has not adopted that. At one scenario, FR-5 makes the product single-use, and the thesis metrics cannot be measured.

## Decision-readiness — adequate
Decisions are stated as decisions and the rationale is visible. The §2 change table explains why each reviewer point changed the design, including the rejected "Call 1 returns which item unlocks" option and why it was rejected (injection surface, correlated failure). Addendum §5 lists rejected alternatives and who decided ("quyết định của người dùng"). The cut table in §3 names what each cut costs, for example "Giả định #6 … **không có tín hiệu trong sản phẩm lúc ra mắt**". Very little here has been smoothed over.

The gap is the biggest open decision: the schedule. Addendum §6 concludes "đẩy must-ship lên khoảng 14,75 ngày, **vượt 14**" and proposes "không để kịch bản thứ hai chặn ra mắt". The PRD body does not reflect that proposal. §1 still says "Thư viện có 2 kịch bản (tối thiểu 1)", §3 item 1 still lists 2 scenarios, and Màn 9 still shows "Anh Dũng … đang giữ 9 điều" rather than the "Sắp có" card the addendum proposes. A decision-maker who reads only the PRD would not know that the launch plan has probably changed, or what that costs (see Strategic coherence).

### Findings
- **high** Schedule cut proposed in the addendum but not decided in the PRD (§1, §3 item 1, UJ-1 Màn 9 vs addendum §6) — The one-scenario launch changes the UJ, the success metrics and the demo path, but it appears only as an addendum "đề xuất". *Fix:* Add a `[NOTE FOR PM]` in §3 that states the decision (launch with 1 or with 2 scenarios). If it is 1, update Màn 9, §9 and §10 item 1 to match.
- **medium** 13.75 of 14 days leaves zero buffer on an estimate labelled "chưa kiểm" (addendum §6) — Several line items look optimistic. "Test set phân loại tiếng Việt (≥100 câu) và tinh chỉnh = 1 ngày" must also reach NFR-7's ≥85% / ≤5% gate. "Eval harness … = 2" includes 20 adversarial runs per scenario plus the guide verifier. Nothing says what gets cut next if the engine slips. *Fix:* Name a pre-agreed second cut, in order (for example: "Buổi của tôi" reduced to list only, or the habit card), and state which acceptance criteria may slip after launch and which may not.

## Substance over theater — strong
There is no persona theater: one learner protagonist (Linh) and one operator (Thanh), and both drive requirements. The Vision could not be swapped into another PRD, because the iceberg, the sealed counter, the replay from the missed moment and the ethical boundary "thực thi bằng cấu trúc sản phẩm, không bằng disclaimer" are all specific. The NFRs have product-specific bounds: NFR-1 call budget, NFR-4 "kiểm bằng test tự động trên chính context đã dựng", NFR-7 ≥85% / ≤5%, and NFR-8 ≤10%. The PRD also refuses to overclaim ("Mốc ~0,6/episode … không dùng làm claim"; "chỉ số tương tác, không chứng minh việc học").

### Findings
- **low** The cost NFR is anchored to a pricing tier that the MVP does not have (NFR-3) — "Mục tiêu là nằm trong mốc giá gói prep-sprint" refers to a package that does not exist at launch, and no initial daily-cap value is given. *Fix:* State a per-session cost ceiling (for example ≤$X at the chosen model tier) and the launch value of the daily cap.

## Strategic coherence — adequate
The thesis is explicit and the MVP scope follows from it. The scope is an experience/problem-solving MVP: "Giữ lõi chứng minh được: tảng băng, cổng, reveal, replay" (§2). Every must-ship item maps to that core, and the cuts are the peripheral items. Counter-metrics are named and pointed (§9, including "tỉ lệ replay rơi vào dự phòng 2 (dấu hiệu kịch bản thiếu hook)").

Two problems remain. First, the metric that would validate the thesis ("Giả định #9: tỉ lệ mở item ở buổi 2 …") has no threshold, and it depends on a second scenario that may not ship. Second, the success metrics measure activity (reach reveal, use replay, second session). None uses the guess-vs-actual data, even though the reveal is built around it.

### Findings
- **high** A one-scenario launch plus FR-5 makes the product single-use, and the thesis cannot be measured (FR-5, §9, addendum §6) — "Mỗi kịch bản cho mỗi người học chỉ có một buổi chấm điểm". With one scenario, every learner gets exactly one session. As a result, "Người học làm buổi thứ hai trên kịch bản khác ≥30%" and Assumption #9 read zero or undefined, and "Buổi của tôi" lists one row. The addendum admits this ("chưa đo được cho tới khi có kịch bản thứ hai"), but §9 does not. *Fix:* Either treat the second scenario as a launch requirement and cut elsewhere, or mark those §9 rows "chưa đo tới khi có kịch bản 2" and add a launch-period learning signal that works with one scenario (see the next finding).
- **medium** The guess/actual gap is collected but no metric uses it (FR-18, FR-38, §9) — The guess screen is kept while the free-text field was cut because "không có phần nào trong sản phẩm dùng tới nó". The same test applies to the guess analytically: FR-38's event list does not include the guess, and no success metric reads it. The calibration gap (guess − actual) is the cheapest direct measure of "you didn't know what you missed". *Fix:* Add the guess to the FR-38 events and track the distribution of the guess-minus-actual gap in §9.
- **low** The waitlist metric is not instrumented (§9 "≥1 giảng viên hoặc trung tâm chia sẻ link") — There is no referral or source tracking, so this can only be observed anecdotally. *Fix:* Say it is measured anecdotally, or add a `ref` parameter to links.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate
§10 is unusually good. It includes byte-identical main-session data after replay, three fixed transcripts for moment selection, per-path gate tests, and the 4-case unseal check on the review page. Most FRs cross-reference a mechanical section. Replay §6 answers the reviewer's request with snapshot contents, deterministic selection, fallbacks, context restore, isolation and an unseal rule.

The weak spots are in the engine rules the replay depends on. They are internally inconsistent in one place and undefined in others, and downstream story creation will hit them first.

### Findings
- **critical** Replay's "3 lượt qua cùng cổng" contradicts the hook-ledger rules for follow-up targets (§6.4, §6.2 item 1, §5.1 Hook ledger, addendum §2) — The primary replay candidate is usually a follow-up item whose hook was dropped at turn *h*. The follow-up rule requires "hook đó ở trạng thái đã thả VÀ `grounded_turn_id` là lượt đã thả hook". The ledger moves every hook to `ignored@t+1` if the next learner turn does not point at it. Code also nulls any `hook_id` that is not in the dropped state (addendum §1). So if replay turn 1 misses, the hook becomes ignored in the branch, and turns 2 and 3 cannot unlock the target unless the persona re-drops a hook. Whether re-dropping is allowed is unspecified. The "3 lượt" promise is therefore mostly one attempt for follow-up targets, and the UJ-1 example hides this because it succeeds on turn 1. §10 item 5 tests selection, determinism and isolation, but not that the target can be unlocked on replay turns 2 or 3. *Fix:* Decide explicitly. Option (a): in the replay branch the target's hook stays "dropped" for all 3 turns (this is a named relaxation, so reword "không có luật nới lỏng nào"). Option (b): the persona may re-drop the target hook in the branch, and the rule for that is stated. Option (c): accept one real attempt and change the UI copy away from "Bạn có 3 lượt". Then add an acceptance test: a sample question on replay turn 2 unlocks the target.
- **high** Hook-drop preconditions are undefined (addendum §1 Call 2: "hook của item khóa mà điều kiện tiên quyết đã thỏa"; §5.2 step 3 "hook line được phép thả") — Hooks drive the follow-up path, the "bỏ qua" ledger, replay candidate selection, the habit card and the counter-metric on fallback 2. Yet nothing says when a hook becomes allowed: which topic, which openness level, whether a hook can be re-dropped after it was ignored, or how many hooks per turn. An engineer cannot implement Call 2's allow-list, and a scenario author cannot know what to write. *Fix:* Add a hook-eligibility rule to addendum §2 (for example: an item's hook is eligible when its topic tag matched in this turn or the previous one, or openness ≥ X; at most 1 hook per persona turn; re-drop allowed/forbidden) and a matching FR-33 schema field.
- **medium** Label semantics are undefined in the PRD (§5.1 "Nhãn 3 loại": `confirm-grounded`: tốt, `boundary-probe`: tốt) — The only definition is good or bad. `boundary-probe` is never explained, yet NFR-7 needs ≥100 hand-labelled Vietnamese sentences and every unlock rule depends on the label. If the definitions live in the brief addendum, the PRD should say so and link to them. *Fix:* Give a one-line definition plus one positive and one negative example per label, or cite the exact section of the brief addendum.
- **medium** FR-21 and §5.3 disagree when the verifier rejects an unlock's evidence (FR-21 vs §5.3, NFR-8) — FR-21 says "Trích dẫn … bị verifier bác thì claim của nó bị bỏ". §5.3 says that when the verifier disagrees with an unlock's label, "người học không thấy gì khác: item vẫn tính là đã mở". The "Đã khai thác" list cites "lượt đã mở nó", so it is unclear whether that citation is shown or dropped. *Fix:* State precedence. Unlock citations are exempt from FR-21 dropping, and the disagreement is logged only.
- **medium** There is no failure path for the reveal verifier (FR-22, §5.3) — FR-11 covers failed turn calls only. If the single verifier call fails or times out, it is undefined whether the reveal blocks, retries, or renders unverified evidence. Moment selection (§6.2 item 4) also depends on the verifier's output. *Fix:* Add an FR, for example: retry N times, then render the reveal with only code-resolvable citations and skip hook-based replay candidates.
- **medium** The reveal-commentary templates and the habit-card threshold are undefined (FR-20, FR-28, Màn 8 "ví dụ ≥2 hook bị bỏ qua") — "Nhận xét cách hỏi, dựng từ ledger theo template" is given one example (hypothetical-future), and the habit card fires "khi ledger vượt ngưỡng" with the threshold only given as an example. Because these are template-driven, the complete set of templates is the definition of done. *Fix:* List the template set (trigger condition → text pattern) and the habit thresholds, in the addendum or as scenario schema fields.
- **medium** FR-33 omits the topic-tag leak check, and NFR-4's test cannot catch a leak through tags (FR-33, UJ-2, NFR-4, addendum §1) — UJ-2 says `validate` flags "một topic tag lộ nội dung item", but FR-33 does not list that check. Topic tags of every item go into Call 1 (addendum §1 "topic tag của mọi item"). A tag that paraphrases a locked item would pass a string-containment test under NFR-4 while effectively leaking it. *Fix:* Add the tag-leak check to FR-33 and name its mechanism (an LLM judge at authoring time, or a human review sign-off).
- **low** The latency gate is soft (NFR-2 "mức khởi điểm, sẽ chỉnh sau khi đo" vs §10 item 8) — It is unclear whether acceptance item 8 blocks launch. *Fix:* Say whether 6s is a launch gate or a monitored target.

## Scope honesty — strong
This is the PRD's best dimension. The "Cắt và hệ quả" table in §3, the Next/Later/Out-of-scope lists, the copy constraint that follows a cut ("Copy ra mắt chỉ được hứa guide dựa trên **lỗi của bạn**"), and the removal of the "1 trong 2 buổi miễn phí" line (Màn 9) are all honest de-scoping. Open-item density is low (§11 has 4 items, and there are about 4 inline `[ASSUMPTION]` tags across both files), which suits a green-light PRD. The gaps are production concerns that are silently absent rather than declared non-goals.

### Findings
- **medium** FR-5's one-attempt lock does not cover abandoned or accidentally short sessions (FR-5, FR-9, FR-10, FR-37) — A learner who ends at turn 1 loses the scenario permanently. An "Đang làm dở" session never expires. Because FR-37 lets running sessions finish, a session left half-done for days also escapes the cost cap. *Fix:* Define a minimum turn count before the session is graded (or allow a reset if fewer than N turns), a session expiry, and whether a resumed session counts as "đang chạy" under the cap.
- **medium** Production data lifecycle is missing (NFR-9, §3 item 7) — The product stores Google identities and interview transcripts for a real launch in VN, but there is no account or data deletion, no retention period, and no statement that these are non-goals. *Fix:* Add a minimal FR (delete my data/account) or an explicit `[NON-GOAL for MVP]` with the accepted risk.
- **low** The daily cap is global with no per-user limit (FR-37) — One user with several magic-link accounts can exhaust the cap for everyone. FR-5 bounds this per account but not per person. *Fix:* Accept it explicitly, or add a per-account/IP rate limit.

## Downstream usability — adequate
This PRD feeds architecture and stories, and the addendum's call contracts, data model and unlock table make it source-extractable. The UJs have named protagonists who carry their context inline. There is no Glossary section as such. §5.1 "Khái niệm" partly serves, but several core terms are used without definition, and naming drifts between the PRD and the addendum.

### Findings
- **medium** Core terms are undefined, and names drift between §5.1, §6 and the addendum (§5.1, §6.1, §6.6, addendum §1–2) — "khai thác", "mở khóa", "mở niêm phong" and "buổi đã xong" are distinct concepts but are not in §5.1. The first two could be conflated by a reader. Hook states are "thả / nhặt / bỏ qua" in §5.1, "chờ / nhặt / bỏ qua" in §6.1, and `dropped / picked / ignored` in the addendum. Labels are `confirm-grounded` / `leading/assumption` in the PRD and `confirm_grounded` / `leading` in the addendum. "Tier" means learner-experience tier in §2/§3 and model price tier in addendum §4. *Fix:* Promote §5.1 to a glossary that includes these terms and a code identifier for each, and use one spelling throughout.
- **low** Assumption numbers point to the brief (§3, §9, §11 "Giả định #6/#8/#9") — They do not resolve inside the PRD. *Fix:* Quote each assumption's text once in §11.

## Shape fit — strong
This is a consumer product with meaningful UX, and the PRD is shaped as one. UJ-1 carries the screen flow and edge cases that UX will need. UJ-2 covers the single operator path via CLI without over-formalizing it. The technical depth sits in the addendum, which keeps the PRD at the capability level ("PRD chỉ giữ phần năng lực"). UJ-1 does include near-final microcopy. That is acceptable for a 1-dev team, where the PRD likely also serves as UX input. No findings.

## Mechanical notes
- **FR ordering:** The FRs are unique and cover 1–43 without gaps, but FR-39–43 ("Buổi của tôi") appear before FR-33–38. Renumber them or add a note, so story extraction by ID range does not miss them.
- **Broken cross-ref:** FR-30 says guide content "đã qua verifier lúc soạn kịch bản (FR-41)". FR-41 is the review-page unseal rule, and the correct reference is **FR-36**.
- **Assumptions Index roundtrip:** The header promises `[ASSUMPTION]` tags, but there is no Assumptions Index. Inline tags appear in NFR-7 (it is unclear whether the tag covers the example or the threshold), NFR-8, addendum §2 ("tối đa 1 item" and the openness values). Several unconfirmed values are not tagged: the 30-turn limit, the 3 replay turns, "20 run adversarial", run-tốt ≥2× run-xấu, and the ≥30-learner read threshold. Add an index to §11.
- **No `[NOTE FOR PM]` callouts** anywhere, although the schedule cut in addendum §6 and the replay/hook rule are real tensions.
- **Success metrics have no IDs** (§9), while the counter-metrics refer to NFR-8. Add SM-n IDs so stories and analytics can reference them.
- **NFR-1 vs §10 item 6:** The acceptance call-count check covers only a 30-turn main session. Extend it to the replay branch, since NFR-1 applies to both.
- **FR-10 gap:** The state between "Kết thúc buổi" and submitting the guess is not covered by the resume rules.
