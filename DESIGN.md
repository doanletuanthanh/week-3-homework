## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for an advanced SaaS simulation environment where product designers, UX researchers, and product managers rehearse discovery interviews against autonomous AI personas. The product’s core mechanic—the "Iceberg Technique"—demands an interface that feels analytical, focused, and deeply encouraging.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Tactical Precision:** Communicates academic rigor and structured evaluation without feeling intimidating or clinical.
- **Empathetic Coaching:** Evokes the feeling of a private, high-stakes sparring gym where mistakes (leading questions, missed hooks, interrupted silences) are treated as measurable growth signals.
- **Editorial Sharpness:** Blends contemporary Silicon Valley tool craft (evoking modern platforms like Peerlist and Linear) with crisp typographic rhythm and modular data surfaces.

### Design Movement: Modern Technical Editorial
The aesthetic fuses **Precision SaaS Minimalism** with **Refined Editorial Craft**:
- **Razor-thin 1px geometry:** Clear visual scaffolding using low-contrast slate dividers and borders rather than loud drop shadows.
- **Surface Elevation Hierarchy:** Pristine neutral canvases layered with warm-slate cards, high-density metadata bars, and tactile pill indicators.
- **Controlled Chromatic Focus:** Deep forest and luminous mint greens act as navigational anchors and progress signals, balanced by purposeful semantic accents (terracotta brick for leading questions, warm amber for hesitation/missed cues, indigo-slate for deep iceberg unearthing).

## Colors

The palette relies on an organic yet high-performance spectrum. Green represents active curiosity, deliberate listening, and methodological mastery.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#0D593F` - Deep Forest Green):** The foundational anchor for high-priority CTA buttons, active state navigations, key scorecard badges, and strong branding accents.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` - Crisp Emerald/Mint):** Used for micro-interactions, iceberg depth progression meters, positive skill mastery checkmarks, and conversational progress indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#6366F1` - Deep Indigo):** Dedicated to technical meta-states: "Iceberg Unlocked" triggers, tacit mental models revealed, and AI reasoning inspector tabs.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Slate Ink):** The benchmark for high-contrast typography, micro-labels, structural borders, and dark elevation fills.

### Functional & Semantic Tone Scale
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for outer canvas; `#FFFFFF` for elevated foreground cards and active interview viewports.
- **Subtle Surface Tint:** `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) for transcripts, metadata ribbons, and inactive state blocks.
- **Card Borders:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) standard; `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) on hover and active focus.
- **Interview Critique Accents:**
  - *Leading Question / Critical Flaw:* `#BE123C` (Crimson / Terracotta brick red) with `#FFE4E6` background wash.
  - *Missed Hook / Surface Stall:* `#D97706` (Warm Amber) with `#FEF3C7` background wash.
  - *Neutral Observation:* `#64748B` (Muted Slate) with `#F1F5F9` badge wrapper.

## Typography

The typography pairing creates an immediate intellectual tension between warm human editorial critique and high-frequency analytical data.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Headlines (`Newsreader`):** Used deliberately for session review headlines, persona psychological profiles, executive summary scorecards, and key conceptual milestones. Its optical serif finish introduces an authoritative, journal-like tone reminiscent of high-end editorial tools.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for conversational transcript streams, dynamic prompt chips, sidebar navigation, form controls, and live dialogue bubbles. Its modern geometric curves offer effortless legibility at dense viewport scale without visual fatigue.
- **Data & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Applied exclusively to timestamps, transcript audio scrubbers, confidence percentages, token latency, and iceberg subterranean depth indicators (`Level 1: Explicit`, `Level 2: Tacit`, `Level 3: Latent`).

### Spacing & Line Height Rules
- Strict `1.625rem` line-height on `body-lg` guarantees high-speed scanning of conversation transcripts without eye jumps across long paragraphs.
- Labels in uppercase format (`label-sm`) require tracking of at least `0.04em` to preserve clarity against dark badge containers.

## Layout & Spacing

InterviewLab requires a multi-panel workspace capable of handling real-time transcripts, persona emotional states, question coaching suggestions, and iceberg tracking simultaneously.

### Layout Model
- **Workbench Layout (Three-Column Workspace):**
  - **Left Rail (260px–300px fixed):** Persona context, iceberg profile tier, persona mood tracker, and scenario constraints.
  - **Center Canvas (Flexible, min 640px):** Active dialogue stream, live recording/audio wave visualizer, candidate question input bar, and dynamic prompt pills.
  - **Right Inspector (320px–380px fixed/collapsible):** Real-time AI coach feedback, leading question alerts, detected bias counters, and unlocked insights list.
- **Canvas Margins & Gutters:** Base margin of `1.5rem` (`24px`) on desktop viewports, scaling down to `1rem` on mobile simulator previews. Gutters between multi-pane cards remain strictly `1.25rem` (`20px`).

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Strategy
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Full three-pane cockpit active simultaneously with zero screen-level scrolling outside the inner transcript pane.
- **Tablet / Small Laptop (1024px - 1279px):** Right Inspector converts into a slide-over drawer triggered by feedback pill badges.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked mode. Navigation tabs shift between "Transcript", "Persona Dossier", and "Live Feedback". Margins contract to `1rem`.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows and theatrical lighting in favor of clean structural boundaries, low-contrast borders, and ambient micro-depth.

### Depth Strategy: Flat Structured Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Solid `#F8FAFC`. Houses the application shell, inactive gutter regions, and background layout containers.
- **Level 1 (Surface Cards & Workspace Tiles):** Solid `#FFFFFF` enclosed with a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border. Ambient shadow is ultra-soft: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Panels, Overlays & Dropdowns):** Solid `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #CBD5E1` border and diffused lift: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Floating Modals & Scorecard Drawers:** Inset backdrop blur using `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` with an overlay tint of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.35)`.
- **Sub-surface Recessed Zones:** Inner chat transcript log and code inspector views utilize `#F1F5F9` with a subtle inset hairline shadow (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`).

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances human warmth with software discipline. 

### Corner Radius System
- **Standard Cards, Panels, and Modals (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`):** `0.75rem` (`12px`) to `1rem` (`16px`). Prevents boxy rigidity while remaining structured enough for information-heavy dashboards.
- **Form Controls, Input Fields, and Action Buttons:** `0.5rem` (`8px`) for tight, click-target visual alignment.
- **Interactive Badges, Iceberg Tiers, and Micro Pills:** `9999px` (full pill radius) to clearly distinguish metadata, persona personality traits, and status counters from rectangular layout containers.
- **Transcript Speech Bubbles:**
  - *Interviewer (User):* `1rem` (`16px`) top-left, bottom-left, top-right; `0.25rem` (`4px`) bottom-right.
  - *Persona (AI User):* `1rem` (`16px`) top-right, bottom-right, top-left; `0.25rem` (`4px`) bottom-left.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** `#0D593F` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-weight: 600`, `0.5rem` border radius. On hover: shifts to `#09422F` with subtle scale transition (`transform: translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary Button:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. On hover: `#F8FAFC` background and `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Critique / Flag Button:** Ghost button with `#BE123C` text, `#FFE4E6` border on hover.
- **Icon Action Button:** `2rem x 2rem` or `2.5rem x 2.5rem` square with `0.5rem` rounding, centered icon, transparent background, and `#F1F5F9` fill on hover.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Iceberg Tier Pills:**
  - *Surface Level (Explicit):* Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
  - *Sub-Surface (Tacit):* Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`.
  - *Deep Subterranean (Latent Pain Points):* Background `#EEF2FF`, text `#3730A3`, border `1px solid #C7D2FE`.
- **Warning Chips (Leading Question / Assumptive Phrasing):** `#FFF1F2` background, `#9F1239` text, `1px solid #FECDD3` border, leading icon indicator.

### Input Fields & Prompt Composer
- **Transcript Dialogue Composer:** Multi-line auto-expanding textarea, `#FFFFFF` surface, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, inset padding `0.75rem 1rem`. 
- **Focus State:** Crisp ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)` with border `#10B981`.
- **Integrated Microphone/Send Bar:** Docked horizontally inside the composer card with quick-suggest chips positioned directly above the input boundary.

### Cards & Containers
- **Modular Data Cards:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padded with `1.25rem` to `1.5rem`. Section headers inside cards feature uppercase `label-md` tracking above high-contrast Newsreader titles.
- **Transcript Bubble Card:**
  - *Interviewer:* Deep emerald background `#0D593F` with white typography for candidate speech.
  - *AI Persona:* Crisp `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #E2E8F0` and `#0F172A` body text. Includes persona avatar badge and emotional indicator chip.

### Checklists & Habit Counters
- **Interview Habit Checklist:** Minimal horizontal rows with custom check circles (`1.25rem` diameter). Completed habits transition smoothly to a `#10B981` stroke and background fill with an animated white check vector. Missed cues show an open `#E2E8F0` ring with an amber dash marker.

### Specialized Iceberg Simulator Components
- **Iceberg Progress Meter:** Vertical gauge visualizing current psychological probe depth (`Surface -> Tacit -> Core Need`). Uses a tiered segmented vertical bar with smooth filling transitions in emerald and indigo.
- **Compare & Playback Slider:** Dual-pane comparison component featuring a synchronized split scrub bar (`#0D593F` track fill with a circular thumb) for evaluating the user's initial question against the AI-recommended non-leading alternative.
